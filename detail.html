Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying, selling and exchanging cars

How does the car exchange process work on SolveMonkey? ▼

You simply list your existing vehicle and mark it as 'Exchange Ready'. When you browse other cars on the platform, you can offer your car in swap directly to the other owner, with or without cash adjustment.

Can I negotiate directly with the vehicle owner? ▼

Yes! All listings with the 'Negotiable' tag allow you to chat directly via WhatsApp or on-site instant messaging to discuss and finalize the best price.

Are there any hidden brokerage or commission fees? ▼

No. SolveMonkey does not charge any brokerage or cut from buyers or sellers. Direct communication is 100% free.
