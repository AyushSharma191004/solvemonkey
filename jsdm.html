FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before you get started.

Is there really no setup fee or hidden charges?
Absolutely none. What you see on the pricing page is exactly what you pay. No onboarding fee, no setup cost, no surprise bills. The 7-day trial is completely free with full access.
How long does it take to set up JSDMCRM?
Most businesses are fully operational within 30 minutes. Import your leads via Excel or CSV, add your team members, configure basic automation, and you're live. No IT team required.
Can I import data from my existing CRM?
Yes. We support bulk import from Excel, CSV, and direct migration from Zoho CRM, Salesforce, HubSpot, and most other platforms. Our onboarding team helps with complex migrations for free.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your data belongs to you. You can export everything in Excel or CSV format before you cancel. We retain your data for 30 days post-cancellation in case you change your mind.
Does the mobile app work offline?
Yes, the field force mobile app has limited offline functionality — agents can log visits, take attendance, and update lead status. Data syncs automatically once connectivity is restored.
Is my data secure? Where is it stored?
All data is stored on AWS India servers with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We are ISO 27001 compliant with regular third-party security audits. Role-based access prevents unauthorized data views.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?
Absolutely. Plans are month-to-month unless you choose annual billing. Upgrade anytime and the difference is prorated. Downgrade at the end of your billing cycle with no penalties.
What support channels are available?
Starter plans get email + chat support (business hours). Professional and above get 24×7 priority support via chat, email, and phone. Enterprise plans include a dedicated account manager and Slack/WhatsApp support.
